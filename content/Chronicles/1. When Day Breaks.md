[[The Party]] finds themselves dazed and tired. Each of them had travelled long and far. After regaining their senses, they discover themselves together on the outskirts of a forest at the break of dawn. With the coaxing and assurance of the [[The Whispers | Whispers]], each determined that the others were of no danger and were, at the very least, allies, if not friends. After some quips and quick introductions, the party set out.

Unsure of where they are and what their purpose is, they travelled to the only visible landmark available to them, a settlement a short distance away. Upon arriving at the settlement, they discovered it to be abandoned and decrepit. Corpses can be seen in the fields with nary a wound in sight, perhaps starved to death or worse.

Upon investigating the tavern, one of the few relatively intact buildings, the party found cultists rummaging through the interior. After the guards were defeated, the cultists were persuaded to leave, but not before dropping a [[Compass Orders | scroll]] containing orders to find an artifact, rumoured to be in [[Nightstone]]. After the battle, they searched the area for a while longer but came out empty handed. [[Bellatrixy]]  was too busy admiring herself in a broken mirror than to look for valuables.

The group's next destination was a barracks that had seen better days. Inside they encountered a Mimic, but was able to overcome it quite easily due to the quick thinking of [[Andre Wahlafell Jon Popoto|Andre]] and [[Thandresa Somner]] who baited the Mimic using mage hands. Once defeated, Andre found the [[Ancient Compass]] and picked it up, pricking himself in the process. Three needles of varying thickness sprouted from the compass, pointing north, north-east, and east. With their stamina depleted and their enemies gone, they rest to the gentle sounds of bagpipes and boss music.
