#### Requirement
- The thrower must be proficient with improvised weapons.
- The creature must be at least one size smaller than the thrower.
- The target space must be within line of sight and within 20ft × the size difference between thrower and target (e.g., a Medium thrower can throw a Small target 20 ft).
- The throw passes over creatures, half cover, and three-quarters cover, but **not** total cover.

#### Operation (all throws)
- The thrower uses an attack to perform a ranged attack to throw the target. A Monk's Dexterous Attacks apply to this attack.
- If the target is grappled, the condition ends.
- This is forced movement: it provokes no opportunity attacks and doesn’t use the target’s movement, action, or Reaction.

##### Willing Characters
- Creature must be conscious.
- The target space must be unoccupied.
- No attack rolls or damage applies and the target lands on its feet.

##### Unwilling Characters
- The target must first be grappled or unconscious.
- The target takes 1d4 + thrower's attack modifier bludgeoning damage on impact.
- If the target is thrown from height, the target takes falling damage (1d6 bludgeoning damage for every 10ft fallen, to a max of 20d6).
- If the target space is occupied, the thrower rolls to hit with disadvantage against the creature in target space.
		- On success: both creatures takes damage (roll once and apply to both).
		- On fail: only the thrown creature takes damage.
- The thrown creature lands prone in the target space, or the nearest unoccupied space.
